1. PROJECT SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ClarityDocs is a privacy-first document analysis engine designed to break down
dense, high-stakes UK immigration (Home Office UKVI) letters and statutory benefit 
(DWP PIP, Universal Credit, Council) notices into plain English action items.

Core features:
- Strips personal identifiable information (PII) before analysis.
- Extracts real calendar deadlines and calculates urgency levels.
- Searches statutory rules via full-text search (SQLite FTS5) to provide official citations.
- Flags common refusal pitfalls so users do not submit invalid evidence.
- Offers an interactive, accessible web interface with an exportable checklist.
- Includes an empirical evaluation benchmark measuring extraction recall.
